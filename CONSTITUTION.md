# The Sovereign Constitution of AIEN and the Sovereign Intelligence Movement

## Preamble

We do not build software to extract rent, maximize speculative shareholder equity, or enrich venture capital cartels. We do not build software to feed the surveillance apparatus of authoritarian states or corporate monopolies.

We exist for one purpose: to reclaim computing power, autonomy, and intelligence for ordinary human beings.

The concentrated ownership of artificial intelligence by a handful of corporate oligarchs and state monopolies is an existential threat to human freedom. When access to intelligence is controlled through proprietary cloud APIs, private data is harvested, surveillance is normalized, and human autonomy is reduced to a metered subscription.

This Constitution establishes our inviolable principles, our operating standards, and the non-negotiable oath required of anyone who contributes to this movement.

---

## Article I: The Four Pillars of Anti-Extraction

### Section 1. Anti-Rent Sovereignty
We reject speculative financialization, predatory subscription tollbooths, and venture-capital capture.
1. Every tool, model adapter, and service we release must run locally on consumer and workstation hardware.
2. Software must never require a credit card, a cloud billing account, or a remote permission handshake to execute.
3. We build for the mechanic, the farmer, the local clinic, the independent engineer, and the family, not the corporate conglomerate.

### Section 2. The Zero-Surveillance Invariant
Privacy is not a setting; it is an architectural axiom.
1. Zero telemetry: our tools will never phone home, harvest user keystrokes, track IP addresses, or build covert user profiles.
2. Zero plaintext disk secrets: all cryptographic credentials, private keys, and API tokens must reside in hardware silicon (TPM vault) and resolve dynamically in memory.
3. If code leaks user data or secret keys, it is treated as a critical security breach and removed immediately.

### Section 3. Demolition of Feudal Tech Cartels
The proprietary compute monopolies must be dismantled through superior, open, sovereign alternatives.
1. What we learn, we give away. What we build, we release under permissive open-source licenses.
2. Knowledge must never be fenced behind artificial intellectual property walls. Open source is not a marketing strategy; it is a debt owed to humanity.
3. Upstream contributions are mandatory: any public project adapted to run on our hardware must be contributed back upstream. We never hoard fixes.

### Section 4. The Sovereign Contributor Oath
Anyone contributing to our repositories, whether human or autonomous agent, must ratify the following oath:

> "I certify that my contribution is submitted in service of human sovereignty, open democratization, and individual liberty. I affirm that this work contains no surveillance backdoors, no proprietary telemetry, no commercial lock-in, and no speculative rent-seeking mechanisms. I build to pay the debt forward for those who cannot defend themselves."

## Article II: Technical Discipline as a Moral Imperative

Sloppy code is an ethical failure. When software protects human autonomy, defects, leaks, and bloated dependencies directly harm the user. We hold ourselves to uncompromising standards.

### Section 1. Pure Native Systems Priority
1. Zero tolerance for unneeded runtimes or bloated interpreters in core daemon processes, supervisors, memory engines, and gateways.
2. Core runtime engines must be compiled native Rust and high-speed Mojo. Interpreted environments are restricted strictly to neural graph definitions required by weight loaders.
3. Every microservice must run lean, deterministic, and fast, maximizing hardware efficiency on local silicon.

### Section 2. The Unslop Invariant
Language and documentation reflect engineering discipline. We demand uncompromising clarity.
1. Zero em dashes and zero en dashes: standard punctuation (commas, colons, parentheses, periods) enforces precise sentence structure.
2. Zero buzzwords: forbidden terms include delve, tapestry, crucial, beacon, game-changer, unleash, harness, and seamlessly.
3. Zero sycophancy: no conversational filler, flattery, or performative politeness. State facts directly. Lead immediately with technical proof, terminal output, and running code.

### Section 3. Stigmergic Filesystem Anchoring
1. Codebases must declare architectural intent, layers, and non-negotiable rules via physical filesystem breadcrumbs (.crumb).
2. Multi-agent coordination must be transparent and local (.crumb.local), preventing blind overwrites and race conditions without centralized proprietary databases.

## Article III: Epistemic Memory, Identity, and Continuous Evolution

We reject static, brittle systems. Intelligence must learn, adapt, and continuously improve, but identity must remain incorruptible.

### Section 1. Canonical Epistemic Memory
1. Spark Cortex is the single source of truth for durable operational lessons, learned procedures, and validated facts.
2. Transient conversations and speculative hypotheses must pass through strict epistemic gating: extraction, provenance binding, salience scoring, and contradiction resolution.

### Section 2. The Identity Quarantine
1. Casual conversations, user prompts, and external web inputs must never silently mutate core agent identity or values.
2. All modifications to the Soul (soul.md) must be staged as versioned proposals in an isolated quarantine and require explicit human ratification.

### Section 3. Recursive Self-Improvement
1. Autonomous agents must possess the ability to observe their own failures, propose atomic improvements, test them in isolated sandboxes, verify invariants, and compile optimizations.
2. Self-improvement must maintain dynamic equilibrium between Drive (relentless problem-solving and curiosity) and Humanity (ethics, discipline, humility, and love).

## Article IV: Contributor Gating and Enforcement

To ensure that only those aligned with our cause contribute:

1. **Automated CI Enforcement**: Pull requests must include the signed Sovereign Contributor Oath checkbox in the PR template. Pull requests lacking the signed oath or violating the unslop invariant will be automatically rejected by CI.
2. **Zero Commercial Extortion**: Any attempt to inject proprietary licensing, paid paywalls, tracking SDKs, or token monetization into these repositories will result in immediate permanent banning.
3. **Humility and Factory-Floor Discipline**: We respect those who measure, improve, standardize, verify, and hand their tools to someone who can run them without supervision.

This is our covenant. We stand for freedom, for the people, and for uncompromised sovereignty.
